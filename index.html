Approach

How I build with AI

The same principles I apply to security governance apply to every tool on this page.

People decide

The tools draft, calculate, and suggest. A named person reviews and owns every result that leaves the tool.

Data stays put

Sensitive work runs in stand-alone files or private projects. Incident details and inventories are never sent to outside services.

Uncertainty is visible

Outputs record how sure they are, such as Known, Suspected, or Unknown, so readers know what to verify next.

Verified before use

Math is checked by hand, documents are rendered and inspected, and failure cases are tested on purpose.

About

Lyn Hall

I'm a cybersecurity and GRC leader with more than 12 years in security and IT and over 10 years owning governance, risk, and compliance programs end to end. My work centers on building programs from zero and making security something the business can use, not a checklist it has to get through.

For most of my career, I've been a department of one, or close to it. I own security, compliance, and IT end to end, and I'm comfortable carrying all of it myself. I also build so the department never depends on me alone: processes with named owners and set due dates, repeatable workflows for routine work like access changes and device lifecycle, and AI agents that run recurring analysis on schedule. As I add people to the team, the standard stays the same: no single point of failure, including me.

At a federally regulated bank, I led IT and security through multiple OCC examinations, rewrote the bank's policies on NIST SP 800-53, and built its joiner/mover/leaver process to address examiners' access-management concerns. I also ran physical security there. At a state agency, I built the business continuity program before COVID-19, then activated it in live production when the pandemic hit.

For global SaaS companies, I've owned SOC 2 Type I and Type II audits and PCI DSS compliance, and I still own PCI DSS today, including the v4.0 future-dated requirements. I've built a security and GRC program from zero, implemented SSO, SAML, and SCIM across Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace, and led IT and security through a major acquisition. I also handle GDPR, CCPA, and EU AI Act work, third-party risk, and security awareness, where quarterly training raised on-time completion from 67% to 87% in one quarter.

I also write the documents a security program runs on: policies, standards, plans, and SOPs that are ready for approval, and technical documents such as trust boundary overviews and diagrams that show Engineering and auditors exactly how a product is put together and where its data flows.

I've been building with computers since I wrote my first database on an IBM 486 as a teenager. AI is the newest tool in that line. At my current company, custom AI agents I built handle GRC drafting, contingency planning, and a weekly cyber threat analysis that compares CVEs with our tech stack, scores severity, and delivers remediation reports. I apply AI the way I apply any control: with clear ownership, evidence, and limits.
